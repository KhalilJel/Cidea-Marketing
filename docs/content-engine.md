# Cidea Marketing Content Engine

- 1 useful SEO article
- Repurpose the article into LinkedIn and Instagram content
- Link each article to at least one relevant service page
- Review quality before publishing

Answer a real customer question first. SEO is the distribution layer, not the reason to publish.
