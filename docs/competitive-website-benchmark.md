## Patterns to borrow, not copy
- Make the agency's own site tangible proof of its design capability.
- Use a clear point of view and strong typography, but keep navigation and next steps simple.
- Show work and explain the idea behind it; clearly label concept work instead of implying client results.
- Separate the offers (websites, branding, SEO, marketing) and give each a clear outcome and next step.
- Keep the visual system distinctive and consistent across service pages.

## Cidea decisions
Keep the editorial ivory/near-black foundation and coral accent. Prioritise stronger service-specific proof, honest concept-project labels, concise copy and direct enquiry paths over adding decorative motion.
