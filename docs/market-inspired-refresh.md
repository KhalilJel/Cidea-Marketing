# Market-inspired website refresh — Cidea Marketing

## Benchmark-derived decisions
- Make the work itself the strongest proof: use portfolio-led storytelling and label concept projects clearly.
- Keep the offer specific: websites, branding, SEO and digital marketing each need a clear next step.
- Use an editorial studio identity rather than a generic SaaS template.
- Prioritise clarity, craft and conversion over decorative effects.

## Visual direction
- Warm ivory and near-black remain the foundation.
- Replace the generic electric-blue accent with a confident coral accent and warm blush surfaces.
- Keep strong type hierarchy, spacious layouts and clear contrast.
- Preserve existing routes, language switch and contact flow.

## Implementation sequence
1. Validate all routes and current responsive behaviour.
2. Apply the palette consistently across buttons, hero artwork, project cards and CTA sections.
3. Review the portfolio and make conceptual work clearly identifiable as concept work.
4. Improve service-page proof and conversion paths.
5. Verify keyboard focus, reduced motion, responsive widths and build.

## Guardrails
No production deployment, DNS/MX changes, prospect emails, fabricated client results or secrets in Git.
