**CREATIVE DIRECTION – Konsept A: «Skulpturell redaksjon» (Sculptural Editorial)**
A high-end editorial studio feel: daring, oversized display typography used almost sculpturally, sophisticated negative space, fine grid rules and hairlines, art-directed photographic or still-image treatment, a cinematic rhythm created with large stills, soft gradients and restrained motion. Think premium magazine meets architecture studio. Avoid boring beige consulting templates and generic agency layouts. Warm or cool palette is your choice; it must feel confident and premium. This direction must be clearly different from a dark avant-garde tech look and from a playful modular gallery look.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Desktop-first, 1440px wide full homepage.
