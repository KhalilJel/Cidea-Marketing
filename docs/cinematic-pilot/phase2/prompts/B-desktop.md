**CREATIVE DIRECTION – Konsept B: «Digital avantgarde» (Digital Avant-Garde)**
Unmistakably a creative technology and digital marketing agency: a dark canvas, one confident signal colour, experimental but legible typography, original vector geometry or abstract spatial art made for this brand, sharp asymmetric layouts and a strong sense of precision and engineering. Avoid generic neon SaaS cards, dashboards, glowing gradients-on-everything and illegible gimmicks. This direction must be clearly different from a light, sculptural editorial magazine look and from a playful modular gallery look – different typography, different composition, different visual language, not just different colours.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Desktop-first, 1440px wide full homepage.
