Focused refinement of this concept («Fremtidig håndverk»). Keep its own visual system – the saturated colour fields, the modular interlocking layout, the original illustrated shapes, the colour palette and the overall page structure – but raise it from a playful graphic website to the standard of an uncompromising, high-end design studio. Apply the same changes to BOTH the desktop and the mobile screen so they stay one consistent system.

1. **Fewer, better graphic objects.** Replace the many small illustrated pieces with a few large, precise, confident signature compositions (especially the hero visual). Each shape should look deliberately drawn, with clean geometry and consistent stroke/shadow logic.
2. **One coherent typographic idea.** Remove the italic serif accent on the single hero word; use the one display typeface consistently for all headlines, and the text face for everything else. Clear hierarchy, generous leading, calmer rhythm.
3. **Concrete Norwegian hero headline – not "Design som merkes. Markedsføring som virker."** Write a new, short headline and one supporting sentence that, within 5–10 seconds, tell a Norwegian business leader concretely what Cidea Marketing does: designs and builds websites, builds brands, works with SEO and digital marketing, and keeps improving it over time. No buzzwords, no clichés, no exaggerated promises.
4. **Remove unclear or unverifiable microcopy:** delete «Bespoke formgivning», «100% fotofri profil», «Helhetlig arkitektur» check-list under the hero, the label «Norsk digitalt designbyrå», «Eksperiment A», «Eksperiment B», «Eksperiment C», «AKTIV MENY», «OSLO», and any other small badge that only decorates. Concept cards keep only the label «Konsept».
5. **Less template feel:** remove most thin outline boxes, double borders, small tag-pills and offset "sticker" shadows; let colour fields, scale and whitespace create the structure instead. Keep buttons clearly recognisable, at least 44px high, with AA contrast.
6. **Mobile composition:** on the mobile screen, give the hero visual a proper composition for 390px (not a shrunken desktop card), consistent side margins, and no text or element touching the screen edges. Long words like «Markedsføring» must never be split.

Keep all other rules: Norwegian Bokmål only, no invented clients/numbers/testimonials/contact details, no numbering of any kind, primary CTA «Snakk med oss», footer «© 2026 Cidea Marketing».
