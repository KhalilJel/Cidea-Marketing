**Overall purpose:** Homepage for Cidea Marketing, a real Norwegian digital agency (part of Cidea). The site is the agency's own flagship showcase. A visitor should think in 5 seconds: "Dette byrået kan virkelig design." After 15 seconds: "Jeg forstår hva de tilbyr." Before leaving: "Jeg vet hvordan jeg kontakter dem." Audience: leaders, founders and decision makers in ambitious small and mid-sized Norwegian businesses who want a more convincing digital presence and want an agency to handle changes, maintenance and further development.

**LANGUAGE (strict):** ALL visible text in Norwegian Bokmål – navigation, headings, labels, tags, buttons, footer, microcopy. No English words or labels anywhere (no "Selected work", "Journal", "Get started", "Our approach", no NO/EN switch). Fonts must support æ, ø, å.

**CONTENT RULES (strict):**
- No invented clients, client logos, testimonials, reviews, awards, partnerships, statistics, percentages, rankings or results.
- No decorative numbering such as 01 / 02 / 03.
- Do not use the word "gratis". Avoid clichés like "innovative løsninger", "digital reise", "vi brenner for".
- Showcase work only as clearly labelled concept explorations: every such card carries the visible label "Konsept".
- Do not repeat the same headline wording across sections.
- Images are placeholders pending license review; prefer original graphic/typographic/abstract visuals over stock photos of people.

**SERVICES:** Nettsider · Merkevarebygging · SEO · Digital markedsføring og sosiale medier.

**PRIMARY CTA:** "Snakk med oss" (alternative: "La oss snakke om bedriften din"). It points to a contact page; no fake booking widget, no email address.

**PAGE STRUCTURE (homepage):**
1. **Header:** wordmark "Cidea Marketing" left; navigation: Tjenester, Konsepter, Tilnærming, Innsikt, Kontakt; primary CTA "Snakk med oss".
2. **Hero:** a short headline with a real point (explore and improve on ideas such as "Digitale opplevelser det er vanskelig å overse." / "Sterke merkevarer fortjener bedre nettsider." / "Design som merkes. Markedsføring som virker." / "Fra første inntrykk til første henvendelse."), one sentence of supporting copy, primary CTA, and a strong visual that shows design capability.
3. **Tjenester:** the four services, each with a precise one-sentence description of what Cidea concretely does.
4. **Konsepter:** 2–3 visual concept explorations demonstrating design taste (each labelled "Konsept", no client names).
5. **Tilnærming:** how Cidea works with a business over time (strategy, design/build, continuous improvement and maintenance) – without numbers.
6. **Innsikt:** blog teaser with three article cards (real-sounding Norwegian article titles about websites, SEO and visibility; no dates or author names invented).
7. **Avsluttende CTA:** short invitation to talk, button "Snakk med oss".
8. **Footer:** wordmark, navigation links, "Kontakt", copyright "© 2026 Cidea Marketing". No social icons, no address.

**MOTION (describe, do not depend on it):** only effects reproducible with plain HTML/CSS/SVG/React (scroll reveals, hover states, subtle parallax), each with a calm reduced-motion fallback. No video.

**ACCESSIBILITY:** WCAG AA text contrast, readable body text over any image, visible focus states, large tap targets on mobile.
