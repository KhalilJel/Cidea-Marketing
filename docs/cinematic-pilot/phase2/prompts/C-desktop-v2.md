**CREATIVE DIRECTION – Konsept C v2: «Fremtidig håndverk» (Future Craft)**
A gallery-like but playful and vibrant direction that must look nothing like (a) a light editorial magazine with serif headlines and stone photography, or (b) a dark tech site with neon accents.

Make these choices visible:
- **Colour as architecture:** large, saturated but controlled colour fields and layered colour planes define the sections (several confident colours working as a system, not one accent on white). Text sits on solid colour fields with AA contrast.
- **No photography at all.** All visuals are original: crafted geometric shapes, cut-paper-like layers, typographic compositions, simple illustrated objects, colour blocks that overlap and interlock.
- **Playful systematic typography:** a characterful, friendly display typeface (not a high-contrast serif, not a wide techno grotesk) used at large sizes, paired with a clean readable text face.
- **Modular, exhibition-like layout:** sections composed as asymmetric modules of different sizes that interlock like a curated wall, instead of a uniform row of identical cards. Avoid generic card grids.
- **Thoughtful transitions:** describe how colour fields slide, unfold or re-arrange between sections (CSS/SVG only, calm reduced-motion fallback).
- Avoid glassmorphism, gradient blobs, pastel SaaS templates.

Extra strict rules for this screen: never use numbering of any kind as decoration (no "01", "Kategori 01", "Fase 1"); the hero headline must be fully visible, never cut off; no promises about response times; no city names or addresses.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Desktop-first, 1440px wide full homepage.
