# Cidea Marketing — Stitch Creative Brief v1
**State:** Working brief prepared by ChatGPT, pending user design approval. It is NOT an approved visual identity.

## The mission
Produce a visually astonishing but trustworthy Norwegian premium agency website. First 5 seconds: "Dette byrået kan virkelig design." After 15 seconds: "Jeg forstår hva de tilbyr." Before exit: "Jeg vet hvordan jeg kontakter dem."

**Business:** Cidea Marketing, part of Cidea. Services: websites, brand identity, SEO, digital marketing and social content/presence. Core advantage to SHOW, not just claim: design taste, technical ability, ongoing improvements. The site is Cidea's own portfolio-level showcase.

**Audience:** Leaders, founders and decision makers of ambitious small and mid-sized businesses in Norway who need a more convincing digital presence and prefer Cidea to take care of changes, maintenance and evolution.

**Primary CTA:** "Snakk med oss" or "La oss snakke om bedriften din". Destination must point to verified Cidea Marketing contact flow when implemented. Never use an unverified email address or fake booking link.

**Language:** Norwegian (bokmål) for all visible headings, navigation, tags and microcopy. Support æøå in selected fonts. English translation can be added later only if complete.

## Required information architecture

- Forside: Brand impression, service promise, what Cidea does, visual proof of design capabilities (concept work CLEARLY labeled where applicable), approach, relevant insights, contact CTA.
- Nettsider: strategy, visual quality, performance, conversion, maintenance.
- Merkevare og design / Branding: visual identity and cohesive brand communication.
- SEO: explain concrete, continuous work without invented rankings.
- Digital markedsføring / Sosiale medier: content, channels and distribution.
- Arbeid eller Konsepter: actual documented work OR explicitly labeled concept explorations; no invented customers or success numbers.
- Blogg: usable blog index and full article layout.
- Kontakt: simple contact and real form / verified address, no fake social proof.
- About/company positioning when useful for visitor trust.

Do not design dummy websites as real client projects.

## Voice and writing
Precise, self-assured, understated, human, Norwegian. Short headlines with an actual point, clean prose; no boilerplate ("innovative løsninger", "digital reise", "vi brenner for"), no English labels on Norwegian pages, no repeated 01/02/03 design numbering, no "Selected work", no "JOURNAL" translated inconsistently, no redundant repetitions of "forstå", "skape", "forbedre"; avoid `gratis`. Never invent reviews, awards, partnerships, income numbers, client names or performance outcomes.

## Creative task: THREE genuinely different concept directions

Design three **complete** independent desktop homepages (1440px) and mobile hero/navigation proposals (390px), with a clear visual signature. Do not deliver three color swaps of the same layout.

### Concept A – Sculptural Editorial
High-end editorial studio feel, daring oversized type, sophisticated negative space, fine grid rules, art-directed photographic treatment, cinematic rhythm recreated with stills, gradients and CSS motion. Avoid boring beige consulting templates. Warm/cool choices can be proposed, not predetermined.

### Concept B – Digital Avant-Garde
Unmistakably a creative technology / digital marketing agency: dark canvas, confident color signal, experimental typography, original vector geometry or abstract spatial art, sharp asymmetric layouts. Avoid generic neon SaaS cards, dashboards and illegible gimmicks.

### Concept C – Future Craft
Gallery-like yet dynamic, very distinct from the other two: vibrant but controlled spatial layers, playful systematic typography, unusually thoughtful transitions, responsive modular visual compositions. Elevated art direction without stock photo dependence. Avoid stereotypical glassmorphism and generic agency templates.

These are **starting hypotheses**, not requirements. Stitch is encouraged to invent stronger alternatives if they better serve the brand.

For EACH concept deliver:
1. Distinct visual story/title and rationale for Norwegian business buyers.
2. Full-width homepage mockup with hero, services, proof/concepts, approach, blog teaser, footer CTA.
3. Mobile hero plus representative next section and navigation.
4. Proposed font pairings (real available web fonts, æøå), type scale, color system, spacing and button states.
5. Asset direction with actual design visuals, not only written moodboards.
6. Motion plan reproducible with zero-cost HTML/CSS/SVG/React (no AI video) and reduced-motion fallback.
7. Example Norwegian page copy and real CTA placement.
8. Accessibility/contrast and image-legibility considerations.
