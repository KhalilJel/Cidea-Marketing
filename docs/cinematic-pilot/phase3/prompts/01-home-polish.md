Phase 3 polish of this chosen concept («Fremtidig håndverk»). Develop THIS design further – do not create a new design. Keep the typeface, the colour system (terracotta, deep navy/slate, forest green, near-black, pale neutral), the saturated colour-field services section and the overall page structure. Apply every change to BOTH the desktop and the mobile screen so they stay one system.

1. **Ownable Cidea motif.** Plain flat rectangles feel templated. Develop ONE memorable, ownable visual motif from the existing interlocking colour planes – for example a precise, recognisable way the planes cut, fold or interlock into each other, with one consistent geometric rule – and use it as the hero artwork. Then apply the same motif systematically in small form: as the accent on each service colour field, on the concept cards and on the article cards. No stock photos, no AI photography, no gradients, only flat colour planes and precise geometry that can be rebuilt in SVG/CSS.
2. **Hero balance (desktop).** Remove the excessive empty space above the headline. Balance the headline block and the motif artwork intentionally (headline and artwork vertically aligned, artwork at full hero height), keeping the headline highly readable.
3. **Remove the artifact.** The small black block overlapping the dark square in the hero artwork is an accident – remove it. Nothing in the artwork may look accidental.
4. **Headline wording.** Always use «Nettsider», never «Nettsteder». Rewrite the H1 so it is not just a list of services but still tells a Norwegian business leader concretely what Cidea Marketing does (nettsider, merkevare, SEO og digital markedsføring – kept up to date over time). Short, specific, no clichés, no promises that cannot be documented. Keep one supporting sentence.
5. **Identical closing CTA** on desktop and mobile: the same heading, the same sentence and the same button «Snakk med oss».
6. **Specific link labels** that name their destination instead of vague words. On the service fields use: «Les om nettsider», «Les om merkevarebygging», «Les om SEO», «Les om digital markedsføring». On concept cards: «Se konseptet». On article cards: «Les artikkelen». Remove «Se arkitektur», «Metodikk», «Kampanjeform», «Visuell studie», «Faglig artikkel» and «LES».
7. **Truthfulness and language audit:** remove any remaining small label or claim that is decorative, English or not documentable. Concept cards keep the label «Konsept». Norwegian Bokmål only, no numbering of any kind, no invented clients, numbers, testimonials or contact details, footer «© 2026 Cidea Marketing», tap targets at least 44px, WCAG AA contrast, no word split across lines.
