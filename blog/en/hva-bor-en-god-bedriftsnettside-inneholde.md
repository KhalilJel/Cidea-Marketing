# What should a good business website include?

A good business website makes it easy for a visitor to understand three things: who you are, what you offer and what the next step is.

## The number of pages is not what matters

A website doesn't need to be big to work well. It should be structured around the questions your customers actually have.

A strong basic structure often looks like this:

- A clear home page
- Services or products
- Proof or examples of work
- Information about the business
- Contact or a next step

## Content and design have to work together

Design creates the first impression. Content creates understanding. Navigation and calls to action make it possible to act.

If one of these is missing, a visually beautiful website can still perform poorly commercially.
