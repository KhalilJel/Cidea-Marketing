# How much does a website cost?

The price of a website varies a lot. What matters most is not the number of pages or how advanced the design is, but what the website needs to do for the business.

## What affects the price?

A simple company website can be relatively quick to build. A larger solution with several services, special features, content, SEO and integrations requires more work.

The most important factors include:

- the number of pages and amount of content
- design and visual identity
- functionality and integrations
- technical SEO
- copy, images and other content
- how much needs to be built from scratch

## The most important consideration

A website should be judged by the job it needs to do. If the goal is more enquiries, structure, messaging, visibility and contact points must be built around that goal.

A cheap website that isn't found or understood can therefore end up costing more than a well-considered one.

## Before you build

Define who the website should help, what they need to understand and what you want them to do after their visit. Technology and design can then be chosen around that need.
