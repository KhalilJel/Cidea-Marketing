# Why doesn't my website show up on Google?

There can be many reasons a website gets little visibility on Google. Technical structure, content, search intent, competition and authority all play a part.

## Start with the basics

Google has to be able to find and understand your pages. After that, your content has to answer what people are actually searching for.

That's why SEO isn't just about adding keywords. It's about building a website that both search engines and people understand.

## Look at commercial relevance

It's better to rank for searches from people who could actually become customers than to get traffic from irrelevant searches.
